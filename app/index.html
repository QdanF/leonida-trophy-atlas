    Roadmap to the platinum

    1. Stage 1 — Play the story (60–80 hours). Finish Chapters 1 to 4 and the final mission. Do the Chapter 1 meditation at Jason’s house the morning after Down to Earth, before Chapter 1 ends. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter; there is no mission replay.
    2. Stage 2 — The 100% checklist (about 100 hours). After the story, open Progress, Stats, 100% Completion. It is forgiving: 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 70 of 80 challenges, 15 of 18 meditations, 300 of 397 stunts, 30 of 40 races, all 42 shooting range golds, all 14 drone race locations, all 8 bank vaults and all 39 gang operations.
    3. Stage 3 — Strangers and odd jobs (about 10 hours). Finish 8 qualifying Strangers and Freaks strands, the five named questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone), Bret’s hunts, and the hidden trophies: the cocktail on Ocean Beach, the UFO night in Kelly County, and the bowling alley valuables.
    4. Stage 4 — Platinum. Vice Got a Price! pops when the other 54 are earned. No online play and no DLC are needed.

    Before you start — 6 habits that save hours

    1. Do the Chapter 1 meditation. The morning after Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in Jason’s kitchen, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept. It is the only missable trophy.
    2. Save at every chapter edge. 15 manual slots, 1 autosave. Keep one save at the start and end of each chapter and rotate the rest.
    3. Scan everything with WAiNK. Hold up on the d-pad, then R1, on every new vehicle, landmark and curiosity you pass. It feeds three trophies at once.
    4. Do side content when it appears. Optional missions and quest givers can go quiet for a while; doing them on the spot keeps the map clean.
    5. Never turn cheats on. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so a cheat save cannot earn anything.
    6. Leave the grind for last. Challenges, stunts and the criminal profile fill up on their own while you play; farm only what is left at the end.

    Honesty box — what we do not know yet

    Three trophies are still unconfirmed: Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess and Ten Pin. Each one carries the method guides are reporting, labelled clearly in its panel, with its source named. Treat those three as leads, not facts, and check back after more players confirm them. Everything else in this guide is drawn from the published guides named beside each trophy.
